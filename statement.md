# Project Statement: Smart Expense Tracker

## Problem statement

Many students make small daily purchases but do not keep a clear record of where their money is spent. This makes it difficult to control unnecessary spending or know whether a planned monthly limit has been crossed. Smart Expense Tracker provides a simple local program for recording and reviewing personal expenses.

## Scope

The application stores personal expenses locally on one computer. A user can manage categories, add/edit/delete expenses, view a month-wise expense list, generate category-wise monthly totals, and set a budget for each category and month. It does not include online payments, bank integration, multi-user accounts, cloud storage, or automatic financial advice.

## Target users

- College students who want a basic personal spending record.
- Beginners who need a simple way to monitor a monthly budget.

## High-level features

1. Category management: view, add, and safely delete unused categories.
2. Expense management: create, view, filter, update, and delete expense entries.
3. Reports: view monthly category totals and total spending.
4. Budget monitoring: set a category budget for a month and see remaining amount or an over-budget warning.
